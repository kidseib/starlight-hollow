Starlight Hollow 0.1.4-beta.1 - Windows x64 playtest

Extract this ZIP before running either file. Choose one:
- Starlight-Hollow-Portable.exe starts the game without installation.
- Starlight-Hollow-Setup.exe installs the game for your Windows user.

Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and a WebGL-capable graphics driver are required.
The game runtime and assets are included; no separate browser or Node.js
installation is needed. Spoken prompts depend on a local Windows English
speech voice. Subtitles are available if speech is unavailable.

This preview is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.

NEW IN THIS BUILD: MOMO'S WISP MARKET
- In the sunny meadow, look for a wooden stall with a striped awning between
  the landing spot and the big Star Tree. A little snail called Momo runs it.
- Aim at Momo until the crosshair turns gold, then click. His market opens and
  the world waits behind it.
- Sparkles you have collected buy three gifts: Star Magnet (40), Pip's Bell (70)
  and Rainbow Blaster (110). Bought gifts show as gold badges beside the
  sparkle counter and stay with you next time you open the game.
- Try clicking a gift you cannot afford yet: the card should wobble and Momo
  should tell you how many more sparkles you need. Nothing should be lost.
- Press Esc or "All done" to walk again.
- Walk up to the Star Tree to see the rebuilt stone altar with moss, a gold band
  and five glowing runes.

GENTLER SIGN PLAYTEST
- Walk with WASD or arrow keys, look with the mouse, and click to zap.
- At a sign with three or more choices, answer wrongly twice. One wrong
  choice should fade to grey so two choices remain, with "Easier now!" on
  screen and a hint spoken a moment later. The right answer never fades.
- Walk away and come back: all choices should be bright again.
- Solve an island and take its Starberry. Its arch in the meadow should
  show a small star and a fainter beam.
- At an unsolved sign, press R to repeat its prompt without losing mouse look.
- In drag-to-look mode, click the circular repeat button and check that it
  repeats the same prompt. Walk away: only that sign's prompt should disappear.
- In Pattern Peaks, try a memory sign. Wait for its demo, begin copying, then
  press R. The lights should replay once in order and allow a fresh attempt.
  Repeated presses during the demo should not add another round.
- Also check launch in both editions, offline voice, save/reopen, and uninstall.
